# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be kind, be direct, and assume the other person is trying to help. Disagree about the work, not about
the person doing it.

## What is expected

- Give feedback on code, not on character. "This allocates on every frame" is useful; "this is sloppy"
  is not.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. This project has opinions written down in `docs/` and `audit/`;
  a decision that goes against you is usually a decision with a reason attached.
- Say what you measured. Disagreement about facts is settled by measuring, not by arguing louder.
- Let people be new. Nobody arrives knowing an event-sourced core and a PixiJS office.

## What is not

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks. Discrimination on any grounds. Publishing anyone's private
information. Sustained disruption of discussions. Sexual attention or advances of any kind.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else the project is
represented.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainer at **ondrej.misak@techfides.cz**, or privately through
GitHub. Reports are read by the maintainer only, and are not shared with the person reported without
your agreement.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, or block accounts, and will
say that action was taken without publishing the report behind it. Serious or repeated behaviour means
a permanent block.
